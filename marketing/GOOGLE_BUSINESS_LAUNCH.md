# EasyRide local customer acquisition launch kit

This checklist is for the business owner. It does **not** create or verify a Google Business Profile automatically.

## 1. Create the Google Business Profile (owner action)

1. Sign in to the Google account that will own the profile at https://business.google.com/add.
2. Search for **EasyRide** first, to avoid duplicate listings. If none exists, create the business using its real-world name **EasyRide** (not a keyword-stuffed name).
3. Select the closest accurate available category (e.g. taxi service / airport shuttle service only if that matches the licensed service and Google offers it).
4. If passengers are collected at their addresses and there is no staffed, signed customer-facing office, configure a **service-area business** and hide the residential address. Use a real address privately if Google requires it for verification; do not use a virtual office.
5. Start with New Plymouth and actual nearby service areas served routinely. Google generally recommends the overall service area remain within about two hours of the base; long-distance transfers can still be described as services without listing distant cities as local offices.
6. Use the real customer contact number, https://easyridenz.com/ and actual staffed/available contact hours. Do not claim 24/7 unless true.
7. Upload the existing official EasyRide logo and real, permission-cleared photos of the actual vehicle and service. Never use fake driver/customer photos or reviews.
8. Complete the verification method Google offers (possibly video). Only the owner should provide sensitive verification materials.

Suggested description (verify every service claim before publishing):

> EasyRide provides pre-booked private passenger transport in New Plymouth and Taranaki, including New Plymouth Airport transfers, local trips and long-distance transfers. Request a trip through our website, see an upfront fare estimate and receive confirmation of driver availability. Cash and eligible online payment options are available.

Do not add prices, discount claims, licensing claims, 24-hour availability or guaranteed pickups unless currently accurate.

## 3. First 30 days

- Week 1: Create/verify the profile, publish genuine photos, confirm the website and phone links, submit the generated sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Week 2: Invite customers who actually completed rides to leave an honest Google review. No rewards or review gating. Ask for permission before publishing testimonials.
- Week 3: Contact a small number of real local accommodation providers and event organizers about pre-booked airport transport; share the partner URL only with their consent.
- Week 4: Review Google Business Profile interactions, Search Console queries and **actual confirmed bookings**; improve the strongest-performing route page first.

## 4. Trust and conversion checks

- Mobile homepage immediately exposes a working booking/quote action.
- All advertised services, minimum notice periods, driver availability and fare rules match the current backend behavior.
- Receipts are sent only after the payment is confirmed and trip completion requirements are met.
- Public business details match Google, the website and social channels.
- Avoid fake location pages, fake reviews, inflated rankings, misleading urgency or mass unsolicited messages.

Google's official guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481 and https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177.
